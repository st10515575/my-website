Naskar Spares is an automative company based in Pretoria,
Selling quality motor spares and accessories to retail markets and wholesale.
Naskar Spares is a family company that was founded in 1978.
They now have four stores located in Silverton, Silverlakes, Pretoria North, and Gezina.
